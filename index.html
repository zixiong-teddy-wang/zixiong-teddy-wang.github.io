Artificial Intelligence · Deep Learning · Generalization

Zixiong Wang 王子雄

Researcher in artificial intelligence and deep learning

I am an artificial intelligence and deep learning researcher. My current work focuses on building a bottom-up understanding of deep learning and using that understanding to explore what may come after today’s dominant research directions, including large language models, self-supervised computer vision, and early-stage world models. I currently describe this broader direction as next-generation deep learning.

My research is also concerned with intelligence beyond deep learning itself: what intelligence is, how it can be understood, and what kind of systems may be needed to realize more general forms of intelligence. I try to build research from first principles, develop a coherent research framework from fundamental understanding, and then use that framework to guide concrete research directions.

I aim to work on frontier problems with as little inherited bias as possible, while keeping the research grounded, concrete, and intellectually honest.

Papers

Deep Learning as Generalization Machinery

From Task-Specific Mappings to Reusable Representations and Predictive Relational Structure

Position Paper / Preprint · Version 1.0 · Zenodo · 2026

This paper argues that major transitions in deep learning are transitions in what models learn to generalize: from task-specific mappings, to reusable representations, to predictive relational structure. It uses this framework to reinterpret the historical shift from supervised deep learning to BERT-style representation learning and GPT-style predictive interaction.
